Purpose of the app

„InkRoyalShop Poster“ is a private tool used only by the operator. It publishes Pins with product images and links to the operator’s own shop on the operator’s own Pinterest account (@inkroyal0887) and retrieves statistics for those Pins.

Data collected

  • Access token for the operator’s own Pinterest account (stored locally and encrypted; not shared)
  • Board IDs, Pin IDs, and Pin statistics (impressions, clicks, saves) of the operator’s own account

No data of other Pinterest users is collected, stored, or processed. There is no sharing with third parties, no advertising, and no tracking of end users.

How the data is used

Data is used solely for API posting to the operator’s Pinterest account and for analytics on the operator’s own Pins.

Sharing with third parties

Data is not shared with any third parties.

Retention

Statistics are kept for evaluating the operator’s own posts and can be deleted at any time. Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings under „Apps“.
